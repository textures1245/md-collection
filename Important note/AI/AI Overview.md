## LLMs 
#LLMs 

A **Large Language Model** is a type of artificial intelligence model trained on vast amounts of text data to understand and generate human-like text. Examples include OpenAI's GPT, Meta's LLaMA, and Google's PaLM.
## LLMs Inference Engines
#LLMs-Inference-Engines

These are tools or platforms designed to **deploy**, **optimize**, and **serve** LLMs for real-world applications. They handle tasks like:

- **Model loading**: Efficiently loading large models into memory.
- **Inference optimization**: Speeding up text generation or prediction tasks.
- **Resource management**: Managing GPU/CPU resources for efficient computation.
- **Scalability**: Supporting multiple users or requests simultaneously.
    

Examples of such frameworks include:

- **Ollama**: A tool for running and serving LLMs locally, optimized for ease of use and efficiency.
- **vLLM**: A high-throughput and memory-efficient inference engine for LLMs.
- **TensorRT-LLM**: NVIDIA's framework for optimizing LLM inference on GPUs.
- **Hugging Face's Text Generation Inference (TGI)**: A framework for serving LLMs at scale.
