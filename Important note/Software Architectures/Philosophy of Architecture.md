## The truth about Architecture 

Software architecture, in theory, aims for structured, scientific, mathematical, analytic, and rational processes. However, in practice, it's often chaotic and uncertain. Architects often struggle to articulate the basis for their decisions. The speaker argues that architecture is "mostly lying" because architects pretend to follow structured methods that they often don't actually use.

- **The Problem of Time, Change, and Uncertainty:** Architectures are typically represented as static diagrams, failing to account for constant change over time. Stakeholders themselves often cannot predict how things will evolve.
